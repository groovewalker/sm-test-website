Our Studio

THE
STUDIO
BEHIND
THE SCORE

Score Maverick is a boutique music studio specialized in animation. We don't do generic — we dig into the world your team has built and find the musical truth that lives inside it.

From sweeping orchestral scores to gritty electronic soundscapes, we build original music that amplifies every frame. Our work has been featured across streaming platforms, festivals, and broadcast networks worldwide.

Every project gets a dedicated composer, a defined thematic language, and a team that doesn't clock out until the music is right.
